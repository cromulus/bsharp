Tap to listen

Listen together, a little at a time

First, use Try the sounds. Tap a color, listen together, and name the color. Begin with red alone if the sounds are new. Add yellow when your child knows red.

In practice, tap the speaker, then let your child choose a color. The arrow appears when it is time for the next sound. After a mix-up, the matching color is highlighted: tap it to hear that sound again. Only the first answer is recorded.

The five stepping stones show how far through the session you are, not how many answers were right. Practice ends at the target you set. There is no timer to beat, and you can stop early.

What this is practicing

The aim is to associate a chord's sound with a consistent color, without needing to read music. Repeated, brief practice builds familiarity. Parents choose when to introduce another color; the app never advances a level automatically.

The published chord-identification routine used 20–25 trials in short, adult-led sessions, several times daily. New chords were added individually, at least two weeks apart and only after the earlier set was reliably identified. Keep practice comfortable; the app's target is adjustable.

This is a practice aid inspired by Sakakibara's study of the Eguchi method, not a guarantee or test of absolute pitch. The study used piano and adult guidance; this app's guitar sounds and independent play have not been validated by that study. Later note-identification work is not implemented here.

Read about the method at Ichionkai Music School.

iPhone / iPad: Enable Guided Access in Settings > Accessibility. Open BSharp, then triple-click the side or Home button to start Guided Access.

Android Screen Pinning locks the app on screen so children can't navigate away.

Enable it:

  1. Open Settings > Security > More security settings > App Pinning (on Samsung: Security > Other security settings > Pin Windows)
  2. Toggle it on. Enable "Ask for unlock to unpin" for extra security

Pin the app:

  1. Open BSharp, then tap the Recent Apps button (or swipe up and hold)
  2. Tap the app icon at the top of the preview, then tap Pin

Unpin: Swipe up and hold from the bottom (gesture nav) or hold Back + Overview together (button nav).
